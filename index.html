ABOUT PROCA

One connected ecosystem for smarter schools.

PROCA is a school technology management platform designed to help institutions organise their digital learning environment from one place. It brings school accounts, administrator access, Windows and Android device oversight, digital safety and learning support into one coordinated experience.

BUILT FOR SCHOOLS

Tools for a smarter school environment.

School Accounts

Register an institution and establish its first authorised administrator account.

Device Management

Bring Windows computers and Android devices into a coordinated school environment.

Security & Access

Authenticated accounts and role-aware access help protect school information.

Academic Support

Support a more organised digital learning environment for school communities.

Progress & Oversight

Build toward clearer visibility of school technology use and learning progress.

Central Workspace

Keep school administration and digital tools connected through one platform.
